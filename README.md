# Introduction

Urban & Co. is a multi-channel retail business specializing in premium clothing, electronics, and beauty accessories, serving a diverse customer base through both online and physical store locations. 
In a highly competitive retail landscape, operational efficiency and the ability to respond quickly to evolving customer preferences are critical to sustained growth and profitability.
This repository contains data analytics projects, insights, and tools designed to help Urban & Co. 
optimize operations, understand customer behavior, improve category performance, and drive data-informed decision-making across all business units.
